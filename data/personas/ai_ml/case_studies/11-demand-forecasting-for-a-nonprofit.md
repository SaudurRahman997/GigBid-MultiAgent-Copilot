# Demand forecasting for a nonprofit

**Client:** Riverkeep Trust (nonprofit)  
**When:** 2025-02, 11 weeks

## Challenge
Riverkeep Trust kept over-ordering slow items and running out of popular ones.

## What I did
- Built a model predicting next month's orders per warehouse and product line
- Combined 120 years of sales history with holidays and promotions
- Shipped forecasts to the planners as a weekly spreadsheet export

## Outcome
Cut forecast error by 43%.

**Stack:** machine learning, python, pandas, statistics
