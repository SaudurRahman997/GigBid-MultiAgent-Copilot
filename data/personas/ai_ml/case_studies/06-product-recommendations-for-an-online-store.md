# Product recommendations for an online store

_Parcelbox Shop · online store · 2024-05 · 15 weeks_

Parcelbox Shop's product pages showed the same bestsellers to everyone. I built a model suggesting products based on what similar shoppers bought and viewed. Along the way I trained on 120 months of orders and served results from a nightly batch job, and finally ran an A/B test against the old bestseller block.

The result: we raised average order value by 63%.

**Stack:** machine learning, python, pandas, postgresql
