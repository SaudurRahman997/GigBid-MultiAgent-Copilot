# Sales performance dashboard for an auto-parts manufacturer

_Castwell · auto-parts manufacturer · 2026-01 · 15 weeks_

Managers at Castwell waited a week for a static sales report. I built an interactive report the sales team filters by region and rep every Monday. Along the way I modelled the data with a star schema and wrote the SQL behind each measure, and finally scheduled refreshes from the CRM export.

The result: we cut reporting time from 8 hours to 10 minutes a week.
