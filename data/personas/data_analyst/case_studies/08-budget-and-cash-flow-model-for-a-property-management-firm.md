# Budget and cash-flow model for a property management firm

**Situation.** Keystone Lettings's founders could not see how hiring plans affected their runway.

**Task.** Keystone Lettings needed this fixed within 15 weeks.

**Action.** Built a driver-based model linking hiring, pricing and churn assumptions to cash flow; added scenario switches for best, base and worst cases; walked the founders through it so they could update it themselves.

**Result.** Cut budget-planning time by 66%.
