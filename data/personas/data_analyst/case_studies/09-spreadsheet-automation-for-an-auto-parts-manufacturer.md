# Spreadsheet automation for an auto-parts manufacturer

**Situation.** An analyst at Ironbark Parts spent most of every Monday copying data between workbooks.

**Task.** Ironbark Parts needed this fixed within 7 weeks.

**Action.** Turned a six-hour weekly spreadsheet routine into a one-click refresh; rebuilt the formulas with lookups and dynamic arrays so nothing breaks when rows are added; added checks that flag missing or duplicate entries.

**Result.** Saved about 8 hours a week.
