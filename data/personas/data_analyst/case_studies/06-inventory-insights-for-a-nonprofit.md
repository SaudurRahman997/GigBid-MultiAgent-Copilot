# Inventory insights for a nonprofit

**Client:** Common Table (nonprofit)  
**When:** 2022-07, 17 weeks

## Challenge
Common Table had cash tied up in stock that never sold.

## What I did
- Analysed sell-through by product and location with SQL to find dead stock
- Built a reorder view showing what to restock and what to clear
- Shared a monthly summary with the buying team

## Outcome
Cut dead stock by 24%.

**Stack:** sql, data visualization, tableau, data analysis
