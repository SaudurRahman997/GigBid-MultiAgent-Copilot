# Experiment analysis for an auto-parts manufacturer

**Client:** Velden Components (auto-parts manufacturer)  
**When:** 2026-06, 20 weeks

## Challenge
Velden Components ran A/B tests but declared winners after two days.

## What I did
- Set up sample-size planning and a standard readout for every experiment
- Reanalysed 15 past tests and found which wins were noise
- Taught the team how to read confidence intervals

## Outcome
Found that 41% of past 'wins' were noise.
