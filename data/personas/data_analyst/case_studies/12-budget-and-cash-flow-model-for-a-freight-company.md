# Budget and cash-flow model for a freight company

**Client:** Portline Logistics (freight company)  
**When:** 2023-10, 17 weeks

## Challenge
Portline Logistics's founders could not see how hiring plans affected their runway.

## What I did
- Built a driver-based model linking hiring, pricing and churn assumptions to cash flow
- Added scenario switches for best, base and worst cases
- Walked the founders through it so they could update it themselves

## Outcome
Used in a fundraise of 12 hundred thousand.
