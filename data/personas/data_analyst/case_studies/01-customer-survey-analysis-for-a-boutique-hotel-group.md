# Customer survey analysis for a boutique hotel group

**Situation.** The Linden Collection collected 120 survey responses but nobody had time to analyse them.

**Task.** The Linden Collection needed this fixed within 6 weeks.

**Action.** Cleaned the responses and tested which differences between customer groups were real; coded open-text answers into themes; presented findings in a short slide deck with clear recommendations.

**Result.** Findings drove 120 product changes.
