# Inventory insights for a B2B SaaS company

_Orbitdesk · B2B SaaS company · 2023-10 · 12 weeks_

Orbitdesk had cash tied up in stock that never sold. I analysed sell-through by product and location with SQL to find dead stock. Along the way I built a reorder view showing what to restock and what to clear, and finally shared a monthly summary with the buying team.

The result: we cut dead stock by 60%.
