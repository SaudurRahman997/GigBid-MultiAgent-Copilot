# Marketing attribution reporting for a nonprofit

_Riverkeep Trust · nonprofit · 2023-09 · 6 weeks_

Riverkeep Trust could not tell which campaigns brought in paying customers. I joined web analytics events with CRM deals to follow customers from first visit to sale. Along the way I built a shared report the marketing team checks every week, and finally fixed broken tracking tags on key pages.

The result: we tracked 31% of deals back to a channel.
