# Budget and cash-flow model for an auto-parts manufacturer

**Client:** Castwell (auto-parts manufacturer)  
**When:** 2022-02, 20 weeks

## Challenge
Castwell's founders could not see how hiring plans affected their runway.

## What I did
- Built a driver-based model linking hiring, pricing and churn assumptions to cash flow
- Added scenario switches for best, base and worst cases
- Walked the founders through it so they could update it themselves

## Outcome
Cut budget-planning time by 50%.
