# Spreadsheet automation for a clinic network

**Client:** Pinecrest Health (clinic network)  
**When:** 2022-05, 3 weeks

## Challenge
An analyst at Pinecrest Health spent most of every Monday copying data between workbooks.

## What I did
- Turned a six-hour weekly spreadsheet routine into a one-click refresh
- Rebuilt the formulas with lookups and dynamic arrays so nothing breaks when rows are added
- Added checks that flag missing or duplicate entries

## Outcome
Saved about 15 hours a week.
