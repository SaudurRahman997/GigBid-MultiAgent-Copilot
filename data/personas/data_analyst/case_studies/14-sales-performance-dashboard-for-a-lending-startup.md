# Sales performance dashboard for a lending startup

**Client:** Quillfund (lending startup)  
**When:** 2024-02, 10 weeks

## Challenge
Managers at Quillfund waited a week for a static sales report.

## What I did
- Built an interactive report the sales team filters by region and rep every Monday
- Modelled the data with a star schema and wrote the SQL behind each measure
- Scheduled refreshes from the CRM export

## Outcome
Cut reporting time from 1200 hours to 15 minutes a week.

**Stack:** microsoft power bi, sql, data visualization, microsoft excel
