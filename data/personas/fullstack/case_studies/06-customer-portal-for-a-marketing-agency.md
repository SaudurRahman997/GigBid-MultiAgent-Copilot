# Customer portal for a marketing agency

**Situation.** Customers of Fathom & Co emailed for every document and status update.

**Task.** Fathom & Co needed this fixed within 16 weeks.

**Action.** Built a self-service portal where customers log in, upload files and track requests; used Django's admin for staff workflows and Postgres for storage; added email notifications on status changes.

**Result.** Onboarded 40 customers in the first quarter.

**Stack:** django, python, postgresql, html, css
