# Online booking platform for a lending startup

_Ledgerly · lending startup · 2025-12 · 18 weeks_

Ledgerly took bookings by phone and lost them when lines were busy. I built server-rendered React pages for fast first load and good search ranking. Along the way I modelled availability and bookings in Postgres with conflict checks, and finally integrated card payments and email confirmations.

The result: we moved 68% of bookings online in three months.

**Stack:** next.js, typescript, postgresql, api integration
