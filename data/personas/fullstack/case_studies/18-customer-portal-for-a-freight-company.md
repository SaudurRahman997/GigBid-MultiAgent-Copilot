# Customer portal for a freight company

**Client:** Haulwise (freight company)  
**When:** 2026-04, 11 weeks

## Challenge
Customers of Haulwise emailed for every document and status update.

## What I did
- Built a self-service portal where customers log in, upload files and track requests
- Used Django's admin for staff workflows and Postgres for storage
- Added email notifications on status changes

## Outcome
Cut status-update emails by 47%.

**Stack:** django, python, postgresql, html, css
