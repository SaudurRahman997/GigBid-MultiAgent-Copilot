# Internal operations app for a property management firm

**Client:** Keystone Lettings (property management firm)  
**When:** 2025-04, 19 weeks

## Challenge
Keystone Lettings tracked jobs across five spreadsheets and lost track of who did what.

## What I did
- Built an internal app with role-based screens for dispatchers and field staff
- Used a PHP framework on MySQL with a reactive front end
- Imported 12 rows of historical data cleanly

## Outcome
Replaced 12 spreadsheets with one app.

**Stack:** laravel, php, mysql, vue.js
