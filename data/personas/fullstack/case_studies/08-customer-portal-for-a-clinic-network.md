# Customer portal for a clinic network

**Situation.** Customers of Meridian Care emailed for every document and status update.

**Task.** Meridian Care needed this fixed within 7 weeks.

**Action.** Built a self-service portal where customers log in, upload files and track requests; used Django's admin for staff workflows and Postgres for storage; added email notifications on status changes.

**Result.** Onboarded 20 customers in the first quarter.
