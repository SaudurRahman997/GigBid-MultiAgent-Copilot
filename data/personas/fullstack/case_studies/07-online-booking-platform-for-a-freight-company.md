# Online booking platform for a freight company

**Client:** Ridgeway Freight (freight company)  
**When:** 2023-09, 4 weeks

## Challenge
Ridgeway Freight took bookings by phone and lost them when lines were busy.

## What I did
- Built server-rendered React pages for fast first load and good search ranking
- Modelled availability and bookings in Postgres with conflict checks
- Integrated card payments and email confirmations

## Outcome
Moved 32% of bookings online in three months.
