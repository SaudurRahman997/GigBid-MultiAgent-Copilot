# Online booking platform for an online store

_Cedar & Salt · online store · 2025-11 · 12 weeks_

Cedar & Salt took bookings by phone and lost them when lines were busy. I built server-rendered React pages for fast first load and good search ranking. Along the way I modelled availability and bookings in Postgres with conflict checks, and finally integrated card payments and email confirmations.

The result: we moved 40% of bookings online in three months.

**Stack:** next.js, typescript, postgresql, api integration
